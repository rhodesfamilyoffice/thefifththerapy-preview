How I can help

Therapy tailored to your story

Every person carries something different. Sessions are designed around your goals, your pace, and what you most need to feel whole again.

Anxiety

Tools to quiet the noise, settle the body, and reconnect with your sense of safety in everyday life.

Trauma

Trauma-focused work that helps you process the past without becoming overwhelmed by it.

Emotional pain

A grounded place to name what you are feeling and build resilience that lasts beyond therapy.

Children and adults

Specialized care for children through play therapy, and for adults seeking clarity and lasting growth.

What to expect

Three steps to starting therapy

A simple beginning, with no pressure to commit before you have had a chance to talk.

1

Reach out

Send a short message through the request form. I will respond within two business days to schedule a brief consultation.

2

Free consultation

A free 15-minute call to make sure I am the right fit for what you are looking for. No pressure, and no commitment.

3

Begin

If it feels right, I will schedule your first session, and the work begins at your pace.
