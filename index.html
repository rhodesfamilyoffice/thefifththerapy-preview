The Fifth Therapy and The Fifth Coaching appear together here so you can choose the right starting point. They are not the same practice, they do not share intake or records, and choosing one does not enroll you in the other.

If you are looking for licensed counseling or mental health treatment, use the therapy pages. If you want structured support around goals and growth, use the coaching pages.

This website is not for emergencies. If you are in crisis, or if you or someone else is in danger, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911.
